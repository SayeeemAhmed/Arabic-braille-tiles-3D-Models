The Oriental Braille Project

Making Arabic education accessible and Islamic learning tactile for blind Muslim children worldwide.

Empowering Young Readers

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn and connect with sacred texts and Islamic supplications independently.

The Oriental Braille Project produces open-source, 3D-printable tactile learning tiles. By combining standard Unified Arabic Braille with durable physical surfaces, we break visual barriers to make Islamic religious education accessible for visually impaired youth worldwide.
